How to Adopt

Follow these steps to give a rescue animal their forever home.

The Adoption Process

1

Browse Available Pets

Visit our "Meet the Pets" page or come to the PAWtners event at Greenfield District. Spend time with different animals to find the one that matches your lifestyle and personality. Our staff will help you understand each pet's unique needs and temperament.

2

Submit Application

Fill out an adoption application form at the event or online. Be honest about your living situation, experience with pets, and daily routine. This helps us ensure the best match between you and your future companion.

3

Interview & Home Check

Meet with our adoption counselors for a brief interview. In some cases, we may conduct a home visit to ensure your space is safe and suitable for the pet you're interested in. This is for the safety and happiness of both you and the animal.

4

Meet & Greet

Spend quality time with your potential new pet. If you have other pets or family members, bring them along to ensure everyone gets along. This interaction helps confirm if it's the right match.

5

Complete Adoption

Once approved, sign the adoption contract and pay the adoption fee (covers vaccinations, spay/neuter, and medical care). Receive your adoption kit with medical records, care instructions, and ongoing support resources.

6

Take Your Pet Home

Congratulations! Bring your new family member home. We'll provide you with starter supplies and schedule a follow-up check-in. Our team remains available for any questions or support you need during the transition.

⏱️

Processing Time: The entire adoption process typically takes 1-3 days depending on the completeness of your application and availability for home checks. Same-day adoptions may be possible during the PAWtners event for pre-approved applicants.

Preparation Tips

Before You Adopt

Research different breeds and their needs. Consider your lifestyle, work schedule, and activity level. Discuss responsibilities with family members and ensure everyone is ready for the commitment.

Pet-Proof Your Home

Remove toxic plants, secure electrical cords, store chemicals safely, and create a designated space for your pet with food bowls, bed, and toys. Install baby gates if needed.

Budget Planning

Plan for initial costs (supplies, carrier, bed) and ongoing expenses (food, vet care, grooming, training). Set aside an emergency fund for unexpected medical needs.

Find a Veterinarian

Research and choose a trusted vet clinic before bringing your pet home. Schedule a wellness check-up within the first week of adoption for a smooth health transition.

Stock Up on Essentials

Food and water bowls, quality pet food, collar and ID tag, leash, litter box (for cats), toys, grooming supplies, and a comfortable bed or crate.

Patience & Training

Allow 3-3-3 rule: 3 days to decompress, 3 weeks to learn routine, 3 months to feel at home. Consider professional training classes for best results and bonding.
