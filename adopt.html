Adoption Requirements

Everything you need to bring a rescue pet home

Basic Requirements

🎂

Age 21 or Older

Must be of legal age with valid government-issued identification

🏠

Stable Housing

Proof of residence with landlord's written approval if renting

💰

Financial Stability

Ability to cover food, medical care, grooming, and emergency expenses

⏰

Time & Commitment

Adequate time daily for exercise, training, and quality companionship

Important Reminders

⚠️

Adoption is a Lifetime Commitment

Dogs can live 10-15 years, cats 12-18 years. Ensure you're ready for this long-term responsibility before adopting.

🏥

Budget for Veterinary Care

Annual checkups cost ₱2,000-5,000. Emergency treatments can exceed ₱10,000. Always have a pet emergency fund ready.

⏱️

The 3-3-3 Rule of Adjustment

3 days to decompress, 3 weeks to learn your routine, 3 months to fully settle in. Patience is key during this transition period.

🚫

No Returns or Exchanges

Adoption is permanent. We'll support you through challenges, but please be certain before making your decision.

What to Bring

Valid Government ID

Proof of Residence

Landlord Approval Letter

Pet Carrier or Leash & Collar

Essential for safely transporting your new companion home

All Family Members Present

Photos of Your Home (Optional)
